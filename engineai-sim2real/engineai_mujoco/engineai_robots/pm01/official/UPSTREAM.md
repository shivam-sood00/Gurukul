# EngineAI PM01 MuJoCo provenance

The PM01 MJCF, OBJ meshes, PNG textures, and ground scene were imported from
`engineai-robotics/engineai_robotics_native_sdk` commit
`83204a459e0e786f855235a8507197496a79acc7` (BSD-3-Clause).

The unused Collada duplicates were omitted. EngineAI's `assets.xml` references
the included OBJ files, so this does not change the model or rendering.
