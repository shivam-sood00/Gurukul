# Velocity Locomotion

Velocity tasks ask quadrupeds, humanoids, and wheeled robots to track commanded planar motion on flat or rough terrain.
This page organizes the task and its supported platforms; learning architectures and teacher/student workflows live
under [Training & Methods](../../training-methods/overview).

## Related Training Methods

- [Locomotion Methods](../../training-methods/locomotion) groups START, REAL, AME, and Contact Trails by how they train
  or represent a velocity policy.
- [Score-Matching Motion Priors](../../training-methods/score-matching-motion-priors) combine the existing flat
  velocity reward with separate, morphology-specific G1, PM01, or Go2 diffusion priors.
- [Student-Teacher Learning](../student-teacher/overview) groups teacher policies, depth students, distillation,
  action priors, Sim2Sim, and debugging.

## Maintained robot families

- Unitree Go2 flat and rough velocity tasks.
- Unitree G1 flat and rough velocity tasks.
- Additional Unitree B2, H1, B2W, and Go2W tasks. B2 flat velocity training is
  `Gurukul-Isaac-Velocity-Flat-Unitree-B2-v0` with experiment `unitree_b2_flat`.
- Booster T1 velocity tasks.
- DeepRobotics Lite3 and M20 velocity tasks.
- EngineAI PM01 velocity tasks.

Use the linked velocity subpages for exact task IDs, agent entry points, and command variants.
