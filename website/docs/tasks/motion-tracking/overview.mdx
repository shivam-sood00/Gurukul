# Motion Tracking

:::info[Available paths]
The task family includes Isaac Lab training paths, selected APEX and EngineAI MuJoCo runners, and hardware handoff
documentation for supported APEX platforms. Open the robot or transfer page for the exact tested contract; support does
not apply uniformly to every motion-tracking task.
:::

Motion tracking tasks train policies against reference motion clips, future reference observations, or motion-imitation
rewards. APEX and BeyondMimic are the implemented training methods for this task family.

## Methods

| Method | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| [APEX](../go2-apex) | Concept, policy interface, and page map for the implemented motion-tracking family. |
| [BeyondMimic](../beyondmimic/overview) | Reference-motion PPO tasks for Unitree G1, EngineAI PM01, and EngineAI T800. |

Use [Motion Tracking Methods](../../training-methods/motion-tracking) to compare the two method families. APEX training,
motion-data, Sim2Sim, and Sim2Real pages are kept together beneath APEX because all four share its reference and policy
interface. BeyondMimic transfer commands stay on the matching robot page for the same reason.

## Implementations

- Go2 APEX motion-imitation and tracker configs live under `source/Gurukul/Gurukul/tasks/manager_based/go2_apex/`.
- G1 APEX/BeyondMimic tracker configs live under `source/Gurukul/Gurukul/tasks/manager_based/beyondmimic/config/g1/`.
- The direct G1 dance task is an experimental dense-tracking + AMP-discriminator hybrid registered as
  `Gurukul-Isaac-G1-AMP-Dance-Direct-v0`; it is not a pure reproduction of the AMP paper.
- EngineAI PM01 has official 24-action dance BeyondMimic configs under
  `source/Gurukul/Gurukul/tasks/manager_based/beyondmimic/config/`.
- EngineAI T800 BeyondMimic config and official policy artifacts live under `source/Gurukul/Gurukul/tasks/manager_based/beyondmimic/config/engineai_t800/`.
- Go2+D1 APEX tracker variants extend the Go2 reference interface with D1 arm reference joints.

The direct G1 AMP task clips normalized actions to `[-1, 1]` and maps that range exactly to the soft joint limits.
Reference rewards and the policy phase advance from the same sampled motion-time offset after randomized resets.
Non-looping episodes end before another policy step would exceed the reference clip, and every reset reseeds AMP history
so discriminator inputs never span two episodes.

Use [BeyondMimic](../beyondmimic/overview) for G1, PM01, and T800 setup, training, playback, and EngineAI sim2sim. Use
[APEX Training](../apex/training) for Go2/B2+Z1 task IDs, notes, and task-specific playback behavior.
