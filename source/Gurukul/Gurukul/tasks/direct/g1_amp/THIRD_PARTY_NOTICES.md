# Third-Party Notices

The direct G1 dance environment is derived from the public `robot_lab` G1 AMP task:

- Source: https://github.com/fan-ziqi/robot_lab/tree/main/source/robot_lab/robot_lab/tasks/direct/g1_amp
- Scientific method: https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.02180

The local task combines dense reference-tracking rewards with an AMP discriminator. It is therefore an adapted hybrid,
not an exact implementation or result reproduction of the AMP paper.
